# Citation

The article title and author order are frozen in `CITATION.cff`. The public repository URL, final release tag, article DOI, and software archive DOI will be added after deposition. Until then, cite each source dataset using the versioned DOI given in `27_英文引言与方法初稿_v0.2.md` and cite the original dataset/source reports where required by the repositories.

Do not cite this working directory as a published article or use a provisional author list.
